Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy informs you about how data is handled within the Cube Burst mobile application. We prioritize user privacy and aim to be transparent about third-party advertising services.

We, the developers, do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as names, emails, or contacts) from our users. The app is designed to function without requiring any personal data.

To keep this game free to play, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and technical device info) to show relevant ads. This data is handled according to Google's own privacy policy.
