Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy informs you about how your data is collected, used, and protected within the scope of the Cube Burst mobile application. Your security is important to us; therefore, the data you share with us is protected with sensitivity.

1. Processing, Storage, and Deletion of Data

Your data is processed in accordance with the law and honesty rules, for legitimate purposes, and limited to the purpose for which they are processed. Necessary measures are taken to ensure that your data is kept accurate and up to date.

Your data, which is stored for the period stipulated in the relevant legislation or required for the purpose for which they are processed, is deleted or destroyed at the end of this period. You can directly and permanently delete your data using the in-game deletion feature.

2. Data Controller

The data controller is the person who determines the purposes and methods of processing data.

3. Collected Data

The data collected by this application may include:

  • Device information (Model, OS version)
  • IP address
  • Unique device identifiers (for Advertising/Analytics)
  • User Profile: User-chosen username (nickname), game statistics (highest scores), and score timestamps.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

Your data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Displaying the global leaderboard and rankings.
  • Synchronizing your high scores to the cloud database.
  • Preventing fraud and securing the leaderboard via score encryption and digital signatures (hashes).
  • Improving service quality, communication, and security.

5. Transfer of Data

Your data will not be shared with third parties without your explicit consent, except for legal obligations. However, it may be shared with our service providers (e.g., Google AdMob, Firebase) and legal authorities to the extent necessary to fulfill legal obligations.

6. Legal Rights

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request correction of incomplete data, or request the deletion of your data. For these requests, contact devmalidemir@gmail.com.

7. Services Used

  • Firebase Realtime Database: Used for securely storing leaderboards and scores.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to analyze user behavior and app performance.
  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements.

8. Consent and Validity

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 14.05.2026 and is updated when necessary.

Data Deletion Request

Cube Burst does not require an account creation (login-free). However, to delete your stored leaderboard data (username and scores), you can instantly and permanently erase it at any time by using the "Delete Data" option inside the game's settings menu. Alternatively, you can send an email to devmalidemir@gmail.com. Your email request will be processed within 7 days.
